# VOXIA Website Transformation Plan
## From WhatsApp-focused UKM AI to Comprehensive AI Solutions Platform

### Executive Summary

Based on analysis of your current website (voxia.id) and business pivot towards broader AI solutions, this plan outlines comprehensive modifications to align your digital presence with new business vision including:
- Financial education platform (invezthink.com)
- Government RAG projects
- AI-based vocational training platform with blockchain certification

---

## Current State Analysis

### Existing Website Strengths
- Modern, professional design with strong visual appeal
- Effective hero section with clear value proposition
- Well-structured problem-solution framework
- Responsive design and smooth animations
- Comprehensive feature showcase
- Social proof with testimonials

### Current Limitations for New Vision
- Hyper-focused on WhatsApp/UKM only
- Limited to customer service automation
- No mention of broader AI capabilities
- Missing education/government/enterprise focus
- No showcase of technical depth (RAG, blockchain, adaptive learning)
